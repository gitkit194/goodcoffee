# Competition: coffee apps in three clusters

About 80 coffee apps found, grouped into three clusters. Numbers are estimates from [appgoblin.info](https://appgoblin.info), not official store data. Installs are cumulative; MAU = monthly active users.

**MAU/Installs** is a rough retention proxy (share of everyone who ever installed who is active this month). It penalises older apps, and below ~1,000 installs it is noise.

## Takeaways
- **Dial-in / logging is crowded:** 27 apps with data and 12 more without. Filtru alone has 76 % of the cluster's MAU.
- **Café finders have no big player:** European Coffee Trip leads with 2.6K MAU; everything else is under 1K.
- **Bean database / "Vivino for coffee" is the smallest cluster with real use.** Leaving out the two subscription businesses (Bean Box, Siip), all "Vivino for coffee" apps together have under 600 MAU. No one has won this space yet.
- **MAU/Installs sits at 5–10 % almost everywhere**, so it only separates apps roughly. Notably high (>1K installs): PUCK YEAH! 14 %, Specialty Atlas 11 %, Cappuccin, Timer.Coffee Android and Siip 10 %.
